What is Modern Research?

A public record of scientific questions investigated using AI-assisted research, computational tools, published literature, and human judgment.

Our purpose is to make the research process visible:

Question → Investigation → Evidence → Analysis → Conclusion

AI is treated as a research instrument, not as an unquestionable authority. All outputs are subject to verification.

Research Method

This is a personal research project made publicly available, not an institutional publication. Research may utilize AI models, literature searches, Python, scientific computation, open-source scientific software, simulations, published experimental data, and human analysis.
