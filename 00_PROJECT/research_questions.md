# Research Questions

Based on initial reconnaissance, the following are the primary questions guiding the project's investigation into CO₂ to solid carbon conversion:

1. **What is the theoretical thermodynamic energy penalty for each pathway?**
   Determining the minimum energy required when accounting for phase changes and separation to ensure the pathway can achieve net-negative carbon removal.

2. **How can solid carbon be continuously separated from a liquid metal or molten salt reactor?**
   Investigating scalable physical and chemical separation mechanisms to enable continuous operation, moving beyond laboratory batch processes.

3. **Is it fundamentally better to use H₂ to strip oxygen from CO₂, or to use electrons directly?**
   Comparing the lifecycle exergy efficiency of green H₂ generation (for thermochemical pathways) against direct electrochemical reduction to prioritize research direction.

4. **Can any of these catalytic systems tolerate direct atmospheric air, or is a separate DAC unit strictly required?**
   Evaluating the impact of atmospheric O₂ and moisture on reduction kinetics and catalyst lifespan, particularly for molten salts and liquid metal catalysts.
